Hepatic / biliary · summary

The signal concentrates on advanced disease

Structural cirrhosis and varices remain the clearest independent markers. Fluid accumulation is highly predictive but mostly attenuates after adjustment. Cholestasis is broad, reproducible, and partly independent.

Renal / urinary · summary

Biochemistry outperforms renal morphology

Azotemia and impaired renal function provide the clearest renal signals. Broad chronic-disease imaging features reproduce well, while named renal morphologies are sparse or absent. Anemia, vascular disease, and cholestasis connect renal disease to systemic complications.

Interpretation guide

Rubrics and analytic choices

Literature evidence

EstablishedGuideline, consensus, major review, meta-analysis, or several replicated cohorts.
SupportedDirect clinical evidence exists, but the exact Glyph phenotype is broader, mixed, or less canonical.
Mixed / emergingCredible evidence exists, but it is observational, conflicting, or semantically indirect.
Unsupported / oppositeNo credible support for the proposed direction, or the usual relationship in the literature is opposite. Non-recovery may therefore be expected.
Previously unreportedNo direct evidence located after structured search. This is distinct from evidence against the association and is not proof of novelty.

Phecode discoverability

DirectExact or near-exact code exists and can be tested empirically.
PartialCode captures only one component or a narrower/broader phenotype.
Proxy onlyNo direct representation; an etiologic or downstream diagnosis is the nearest comparator.
Not discoverableNo meaningful Phecode exists in the baseline mapping.

Association counting

Counts are restricted to the active scope: 11 hepatic/biliary, 13 renal/urinary, 28 cardiovascular, or the full 141-outcome systemic panel. A Discovery association must pass Bonferroni correction across all 601,050 Glyph pairs and have OR > 1. Held-out reproduction uses each site’s BH threshold and preserved direction.

Laboratory concepts have no External Site 2 estimates. They are displayed as N/A and that site is excluded from their ranking denominator.

Confounder-adjusted reading

DML flags are reported independently at each cohort using the cohort-specific BH threshold. Failure to pass is described as attenuation unless estimates consistently collapse with reasonable precision. No row is called causal.

Extreme DML estimates and very wide intervals require caution because quasi-separation is more frequent on held-out cohorts.

Peri-index Phecode baseline

The comparison uses phecode_links.parquet, filtered to window == "recent" after the repeat-event bug fix. Exposure window: . Phecode associations are displayed with the same unadjusted D/I/E1 count structure as Glyph; External Site 2 and confounder-adjusted Phecode estimates are unavailable.

Selection into this page

Main rows were limited to broad, highly replicated, strongly adjusted, high-effect, or clinically distinctive findings. Sparse random associations were excluded; additional significant concepts remain available in collapsed accounting tables.

This avoids treating every statistically significant pair as equivalent clinical evidence.
